# Welcome to Quantome's Data Refinement Codebase 💎

Quantome is a Paris-based company founded in 2013, dedicated to bioinformatics and data-intensive applications. The prefix ‘quant’ and the suffix ‘ome’ represent quantitative analysis of biological molecules or entities in an organism. We govern data soundly by keeping digital assets free from infrastructure bloat, archaic standards, and vendor lock-in. To demonstrate and uphold these principles, we share our open-source codebase for free and stay committed to keeping it thin.
